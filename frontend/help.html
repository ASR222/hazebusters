01

How it works

  1. Who and where. You pick school (7 AM–5 PM), outdoor worker (6 AM–7 PM) or general (6 AM–9 PM), then share your location or choose a city.
  2. Your clip. The app asks the API for a one-time upload link and sends your clip (MP4, WebM or MOV, up to 60 s and 100 MB) straight to Amazon S3, showing progress.
  3. The number. The backend looks up the nearest WAQI station and the CAMS forecast via Open-Meteo. A station within 25 km and updated in the last 3 hours is used; otherwise the CAMS value, scaled ×1.25. Both become a CPCB-style AQI from PM2.5 and PM10.
  4. How sure. Confidence is high when a station within 25 km agrees with CAMS on the band, medium when only one of those holds, and low otherwise or when only the model is available.
  5. The decision. The worst 3-hour slot left in your hours sets the call: AQI ≤ 100 is "Outdoors OK", 101–200 "Limit outdoor time", above 200 "Avoid outdoors". The earliest good slot becomes the best window, today or tomorrow.
  6. The clip check (experimental). A quality gate drops dark, blurry or sky-less frames; a classifier flags possible smoke or fire with a heatmap, and YOLO11n counts vehicles. This never changes the AQI.
  7. Advice. Amazon Bedrock phrases the computed numbers in English and Hindi (template fallback), and Amazon Polly reads them aloud. You can share the summary or send it on WhatsApp.

03

Setup

Install it on your phone

HazeBusters is a web app. In Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, use Share → "Add to Home Screen".

Run the frontend locally

cd frontend
python3 -m http.server 8000

Open http://localhost:8000. Location works on localhost and HTTPS only; on a phone over plain LAN HTTP, use the city list.

Preview a saved result

Put a result in frontend/mock/result.json and open http://localhost:8000/?mock=1.

Deploy

Set API_BASE in config.js, bump CACHE_VERSION in sw.js, zip the contents of frontend/ and upload it to Amplify Hosting. The full commands are in frontend/README.md.

Troubleshooting

"Location permission denied" or "Couldn't get your location"

Allow location for this site in your browser settings, or pick the nearest city from the list. Location only works on HTTPS pages.

"Unsupported video type"

Use an MP4, WebM or MOV clip. Most phone cameras record MP4 or MOV by default.

"Clip is … MB" or "Clip is … s"

The limits are 100 MB and 60 s. Record about 10 seconds instead; it's faster and enough for the check.

"Upload failed" or "Network error"

Check your connection and tap Retry. Mobile data with a weak signal can drop large uploads, so a shorter clip helps.

"Analysis took longer than 90 s"

The server was busy or slow to start. Tap Retry; the second try is usually quicker.

"Couldn't check this clip"

Too few frames passed the quality gate (too dark, too blurry, or no sky in view). Go outdoors and point at open sky and street. The AQI and decision are still shown.

I still see an old version of the app

Reload once more, or close and reopen the tab. The app keeps an offline copy that refreshes in the background.

About the data

Stations

Live readings from the World Air Quality Index project. Used when a station is within 25 km and reported in the last 3 hours.

Forecast model

CAMS (Copernicus) via Open-Meteo, on a ~45 km grid, in 3-hour slots for today and tomorrow. When it stands in for a station, it is scaled ×1.25 because it reads low against Indian ground stations.

AQI

CPCB-style categories (Good to Severe) computed from PM2.5 and PM10.

What it can't do

  • It isn't the official 24-hour, multi-pollutant AQI.
  • The model is coarse (~45 km) and its correction was fitted on three cities.
  • The smoke check can mistake city haze or bright sky for smoke. It's a hint only.
  • It isn't medical advice or an official notice.
