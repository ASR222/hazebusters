The mission

Most towns have no monitor. People still have to decide.

From Ranchi city, the nearest official air station is about 110 km away, and big-city stations go quiet too. A school deciding whether today's assembly can be outdoors usually has nothing to go on.

HazeBusters fills the gap with the best number it can find, and is honest about how good that number is.

Why it matters

Why haze gets worse in winter.

  1. Sources add particlesTraffic, cooking and heating fires, industry, dust and crop-residue burning all put fine particles into the air.
  2. Still winter air holds them inOn cold, calm days a warmer layer can sit above the ground like a lid, so pollution builds up instead of mixing away.
  3. Fine particles go deepPM2.5 is small enough to reach deep into the lungs, which is why AQI and outdoor time matter most for children and people with asthma.
  • 99%of people worldwide breathe air above WHO guideline limits (2019 data). WHO
  • 5 µg/m³WHO's annual guideline for PM2.5 (2021). WHO fact sheet
  • 60 µg/m³India's 24-hour PM2.5 standard (NAAQS 2009). CPCB

Help

Questions, answered.

Short answers about the number, the clip and your location.

Open the help guide
Where does the AQI number come from?

From the nearest WAQI station when it's within 25 km and updated in the last 3 hours. Otherwise from the CAMS forecast model (about a 45 km grid) via Open-Meteo. The result always says which one was used.

Is this the official AQI?

No. It's a CPCB-style AQI computed from hourly PM2.5 and PM10, not the official 24-hour, multi-pollutant index. Treat it as a good guide, not a notice.

Why do you need a video?

The clip is checked for possible smoke, fire and traffic as a hint about local sources. It never changes the AQI or the decision.

"Use my location" isn't working.

Browsers only share location on secure (HTTPS) pages and after you allow it. If it's blocked, pick the nearest city from the list instead.

What does "confidence" mean?

How much to trust the number. High when a station within 25 km agrees with the CAMS model on the band; medium when only one of those is true; low otherwise, or when only the model is available.

About the data

Stations

Live readings from the World Air Quality Index project. Used when a station is within 25 km and reported in the last 3 hours.

Forecast model

CAMS (Copernicus) via Open-Meteo, on a ~45 km grid, in 3-hour slots for today and tomorrow. When it stands in for a station, it is scaled ×1.25 because it reads low against Indian ground stations.

AQI

CPCB-style categories (Good to Severe) computed from PM2.5 and PM10.

What it can't do

  • It isn't the official 24-hour, multi-pollutant AQI.
  • The model is coarse (~45 km) and its correction was fitted on three cities.
  • The smoke check can mistake city haze or bright sky for smoke. It's a hint only.
  • It isn't medical advice or an official notice.
